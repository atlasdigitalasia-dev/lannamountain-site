The foundations are fine. The site is on HTTPS with clean redirects, robots.txt and the Yoast sitemap are correct, every page self-canonicalises, and the homepage has a sensible title and H1 targeting "painters and decorators in Bristol". The business also has what most competitors would pay for: a perfect 5.0 from 33 Google reviews, a Checkatrade profile, and 15+ detailed, named testimonials.

The website and the listing aren't turning that reputation into visibility. The site is only 7 URLs. All the services sit on one 255-word page, there are no service or area pages, and the "Areas We Cover" page literally reads "No areas available at the moment." Only 4 pages are indexed. Ten internal links on the homepage, plus the logo and several images on every page, still point at the developer's temporary staging server, which blocks Google. On Google Maps, the listing doesn't make the top 40 for any Bristol term, while competitors with fewer or worse reviews sit in the top 10.

Overall assessment: critical issues. A business with this reputation should be in the Bristol Maps results and isn't. The priority order below puts the fastest route to enquiries first (Maps and indexation), then the pages that win organic rankings.

Top 3 priorities Fastest route to enquiries first
01 · Critical · Technical

Remove every staging-server reference and get all pages indexed

Ten homepage links (Get a Free Quote, View Our Services, Read Reviews and others) and the logo and images on every page point to azpaintinganddecorating-co-uk.zeekservers.co.uk. That server's robots.txt says "Disallow: /". Search-and-replace the staging URL across the WordPress database, then submit the 7 sitemap URLs in Search Console and request indexing for Services, Gallery and Testimonials.

02 · Critical · Local / Maps

Fix the Google Business Profile so 33 five-star reviews actually rank

The listing is absent from the top 40 in Maps for all 10 terms. Fix the website link first: it points to http://azpaintinganddecorating.co.uk/, which takes two redirects, so update it to https://www.azpaintinganddecorating.co.uk/. Then fill out the service areas (actual Bristol districts), services, description and photos, and match the categories used by the listings that do rank (all "Painter and Decorator"). Add LocalBusiness/HousePainter schema to the site with the same name, phone and area.

03 · Content

Build dedicated service and area pages

Every competitor ranking organically has separate pages for exterior, interior and commercial work, and the strongest (bristolpainters.co.uk, #1 for the core term) also has 5 area pages. Split Services into Exterior Painting Bristol, Interior Painting & Decorating Bristol and Commercial Painting Bristol. Replace the empty Areas page with real coverage and individual pages for the main districts AZ works in.

Overall verdict

Great reputation, invisible website. Fixable without a rebuild.

Nothing here needs a new site. The two critical fixes (clearing the staging-server references and correcting the Google Business Profile) are days of work, not months. They remove what is currently stopping Google from crawling the site properly and ranking the listing. The service and area pages are the bigger job, and they are what moves organic rankings. Competitors with fewer reviews and thinner sites are outranking AZ purely on structure. Order of work: technical clean-up and GBP this month, then service pages, then area pages.

⛔

The main conversion buttons send visitors through a staging server

On the homepage, "Get a Free Quote", "View Our Services", "More Information", "View Gallery", "Read Reviews", "Contact us" and both "GET A FREE QUOTE" buttons all link to azpaintinganddecorating-co-uk.zeekservers.co.uk. Some bounce back to the live site, but /services/ on the staging host serves a full copy of the page with a 200 status. Visitors can end up browsing a temporary development copy, and Google treats the links as pointing to a host it is told not to crawl. Fix: a WordPress search-and-replace of the staging URL for the live https://www URL, across the database and theme options.

Brand check: the site doesn't rank for its own name

This sits outside the local tally. A search for "az painting and decorating" (Bristol) shows AZ's knowledge panel (5.0★, 33 reviews), but the website isn't in the 10 organic results. Companies House, Checkatrade and BookaBuilderUK appear instead, alongside unrelated "A-Z Painting" firms. The site does surface at #2 when the domain name itself is searched, so it is indexed. It just isn't trusted enough to rank for the brand.

The pattern

Invisible on every Bristol term, in both search and Maps.

There is no "wins on X, loses on Y" here. AZ has no first-page presence on any of the 10 local terms and no top-40 Maps position on any of them either. The sites that do rank are a mix of directories (Checkatrade appears on 9 of the 10 page-1 results) and local decorators with dedicated pages for the exact service searched. Those directories are a short-term route in: AZ already has a Checkatrade profile, and making it complete and review-rich captures some of that traffic while the site is fixed. The long-term fix is the page structure in Section 05.

Key insight

The gap is structural, not reputational.

Clifton Bristol Decorators has exactly the same number of Google reviews as AZ (33), at a lower rating (4.5 vs 5.0). It sits at #5–7 in Maps for every term checked and ranks organically for the core term. AZ appears nowhere. The difference is the website behind the listing: separate interior, exterior and commercial pages, meta descriptions, alt text, and no staging-server leakage. Mac Decorators Bristol shows the same thing from the other side. Its one-page site has no meta description and a page title of "Website", yet with 43 reviews it reaches the Maps top 25 on all 10 terms and organic page 1 for 3. AZ doesn't need more reviews to compete. It needs the technical clean-up and the service pages in this report.
